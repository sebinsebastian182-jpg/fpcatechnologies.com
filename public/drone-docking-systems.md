# Custom Drone Docking Systems from FPCA Technologies

FPCA Technologies Private Limited designs, develops and integrates custom drone docking stations and related docking systems for customer UAV operations.

Official service page: https://www.fpcatechnologies.com/drone-docking-systems

Contact: admin@fpcatechnologies.com

Location: Maker Village, KINFRA Hi-Tech Park, Kalamassery, Kochi, Kerala, India

## Service summary

FPCA works with customers that need a docking station or docking-related system for a drone. Each engagement begins with the customer's aircraft, mission, deployment environment and required docking functions. FPCA then defines an appropriate project scope with the customer.

## Potential project scope

- Mechanical docking concepts and docking-station architecture
- Power or charging integration when required
- Alignment, landing, sensing and telemetry requirements
- Docking-station controls
- Integration with the customer's drone, autopilot and operating workflow
- Prototype development, testing and iteration

These are requirement-dependent engineering capabilities, not a claim that every feature is available as a standard off-the-shelf product.

## Relevant applications

- Inspection and monitoring
- Security and surveillance
- Industrial and infrastructure sites
- Agriculture and remote field operations
- Research, OEM and custom UAV programmes
